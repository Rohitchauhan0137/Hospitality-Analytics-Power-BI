# 🏨 Hospitality Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Hospitality Analysis** project is a Power BI dashboard designed to analyze hotel booking performance and identify important business insights.

The dashboard focuses on key hospitality metrics such as **Revenue, Bookings, Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, Cancellations, No-Shows, and Revenue Leakage**.

The objective of this project is to understand hotel performance, identify revenue opportunities, and analyze differences between properties, weekdays and weekends, and booking outcomes.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall hotel revenue and booking performance
* Monitor occupancy and room utilization
* Analyze ADR and RevPAR
* Understand booking cancellations and no-shows
* Compare weekday and weekend performance
* Identify revenue leakage
* Compare performance across different hotels
* Analyze booking length of stay
* Identify business opportunities from the dashboard

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Overall Hotel Performance

The dashboard shows **57.87% overall occupancy** and approximately **₹170.9 Cr in realized revenue** from around **135K bookings**.

### 2. Revenue Leakage

Approximately **₹200.8 Cr revenue was generated**, while around **₹170.9 Cr was realized**.

This creates a revenue gap of approximately **₹29.9 Cr**, representing around **14.9% of generated revenue**.

The dashboard indicates that cancellations, no-shows, booking modifications, refunds, discounts, or differences between booked and realized values could be areas for further investigation.

### 4. Booking Status

The dashboard shows approximately:

* **70% Checked Out**
* **25% Cancelled**
* **5% No Show**

Cancellations represent a significant portion of bookings and can therefore be analyzed further to understand their impact on revenue realization.

---

### 5. Weekday vs Weekend Analysis

The dashboard shows a significant difference between weekday and weekend occupancy:

* **Weekend Occupancy:** 73.58%
* **Weekday Occupancy:** 51.34%

This indicates a clear difference in demand between weekdays and weekends.

---

### 6. Length of Stay

The length-of-stay analysis shows that the largest booking groups are concentrated in shorter stays.

This creates an opportunity to explore **longer-stay packages, weekday extensions, and "stay one more night" offers** to improve room utilization.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1. **Reduce Revenue Leakage**
   Track leakage by hotel, booking status, platform, room class, and month.

2. **Analyze Cancellations**
   Study cancellation reasons and booking lead time.

3. **Increase Weekday Occupancy**
   Explore corporate packages, weekday offers, and business partnerships.

4. **Reduce No-Shows**
   Use pre-arrival reminders and booking reconfirmation.

5. **Optimize Hotel Pricing**
   Monitor ADR and RevPAR together with occupancy.

6. **Increase Length of Stay**
   Test longer-stay packages and one-night extension offers.

7. **Analyze Booking Platforms**
   Compare platforms using booking volume, cancellation rate, realization rate, ADR, and revenue leakage.
